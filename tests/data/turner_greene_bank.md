# Turner & Greene (1977) — Bank of Worked Propositional-Analysis Examples

**Source:** Turner, A., & Greene, E. (1977). *The Construction and Use of a Propositional Text Base.*
Technical Report No. 63, Institute for the Study of Intellectual Behavior, University of Colorado.
https://www.colorado.edu/ics/sites/default/files/attached-files/77-63.pdf

Extracted from the scanned page images (the PDF has no text layer). Page numbers are the
**printed report page numbers** (in the scan, PNG index = page + 4 up to p. 46, and page + 6
from p. 47 on, because pages 45-46 were scanned twice).

## Notes

- Proposition counts are the number of propositions listed in the printed analysis. Where the
  manual prints one proposition with another embedded inside it (e.g. `(QUALITY OF, SALLY, (RED, HAIR))`),
  the count follows the printed list, and the embedding is described in the notes. A convention that
  unpacks embedded propositions (as Kintsch's counts usually do) would yield higher numbers for a few items.
- Several examples have explicit alternative analyses ("or", "&/OR") or alternative branches for
  ambiguous connectives; these are recorded in the notes rather than forced into a single count.
- Example **48** is starred in the manual as an unacceptable sentence (it illustrates a constraint
  violation) but still has a 3-proposition analysis.
- Examples 26, 49, and 50 are numbered in the manual but have no propositional analysis
  (26 is deferred to the Contrast section, where it reappears as example 57; 49-50 illustrate
  disjunction in prose only). They are therefore not entries in this bank.
- The Section 3.4 passage is an excerpt from Ernest Hemingway's *The Sun Also Rises* (copyrighted
  fiction), so its text is identified but not transcribed here; its 57-proposition template text
  base is summarized in the entry's notes.

## Relation to Brown et al. (2008) "69 examples"

Brown, Snodgrass, Kemper, Herman & Covington (2008, *Behavior Research Methods* 40:540-545)
validated CPIDR 3 against "69 examples from Turner & Greene (1977)". The manual's numbered
examples in Section 2 run exactly 1 through 69, which is almost certainly the source of that
figure. This bank contains 68 entries for Section 2, not 69, because: (a) numbered examples
26, 49, and 50 carry no analysis and are excluded; (b) lettered variants add entries — 38a/38b
and 65a/65b are analyzed separately (2 entries each), while 18a-k, 52a-f, 54a-d, 55a-e, and 56b-e
are surface paraphrases sharing one analysis (1 entry each). Net: 66 base numbers + 2 extra
lettered analyses = 68. The bank's total of 73 entries adds the three Section 3 worked passages
and the two Section 4.3 scoring examples.
